- **Observations:**
  - **This changes what the accuracy question even is.** "Does steering raise accuracy?" was the wrong question because it pools two populations. The right question is "can we identify the decoy-damaged items *without* the answer?" — because on those items the write already works. `flippable` is defined from ground truth (L0 right, L1b wrong), so **targeting on it is oracle targeting and not deployable**; the result is not a rescue claim, it is a statement that a *selector* is the missing component.
  - **And the selector is exactly what the other two instruments are for.** If Instrument 1's `A_id`/`A_struct` or Instrument 2's ISF/NLA flags predict flippability *without* the answer, steering becomes deployable at the measured effect size. **This promotes E7 triangulation from a positioning exercise to the load-bearing experiment of the accuracy arm** — a much better reason to run it than "the instruments should agree".

A 0.78 parse rate sat unremarked through an entire accuracy run and its results entry.
