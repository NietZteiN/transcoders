### Target Date: 2026-09-09 (PRE-REGISTRATION Phase B — NLA pairs at every layer of Gemma-3-4B-it, and the multi-layer gate)

- **Hypotheses / what we're testing:** User direction (2026-09-08): *"let's try maximum GPU usage, smaller Gemma model, train nlas on all layers."* This entry freezes, before any SFT job is submitted, (i) what is trained, (ii) how each trained pair is checked, (iii) the multi-layer gate, verbatim from [`../../docs/nla_multilayer_scoping.md`](../../docs/nla_multilayer_scoping.md) §4 (frozen 2026-09-07), with the one thing that doc left open — *which* layers form the multi-layer arm — now fixed from H-W31's verdict, and (iv) a new per-layer depth hypothesis that only an all-layer sweep can answer.
  - **What is trained.** One AV (verbaliser) + one AR (reconstructor) per layer K ∈ 0..33 of `google/gemma-3-4b-it` (34 layers, d 2560; global-attention layers 5/11/17/23/29, window 1024), 68 models. Layer convention: `layer_index K` = output of decoder block K = HF `hidden_states[K+1]`, captured by forward hooks (never the last `output_hidden_states` entry, which is post-final-norm — measured 139 vs ≈ 79 000 pre-norm on this host). Host loads through a **text-only checkpoint** (`data/nla/ml/gemma4b/host_text`, `Gemma3ForCausalLM`, verified 2026-09-08 bit-identical hidden states to the multimodal path at all 35 indices — `Gemma3ForCausalLM.from_pretrained(hub id)` silently loads nothing). Recipe = the vendored `nla-repo` SFT stage re-implemented in plain PyTorch (`nla/src/nla_train.py`), RL stage skipped; deviations from the recipe are marked `# DEV:` in [`../../nla/configs/nla_ml.yaml`](../../nla/configs/nla_ml.yaml) and listed under Setup.
  - **Per-pair checks (frozen; a pair failing any is reported as `PAIR-DEAD` at that layer and excluded from the multi-layer arms, never silently kept):** (a) AV held-out loss with the row's own vector must be **lower than with a permuted vector** (`holdout_gap_permuted_minus_real > 0`); (b) AR held-out **fve ≥ 0.20** and above both the shuffled-target fve and the train-mean baseline; (c) on 96 greedy reads of held-out vectors, `n_no_tags ≤ 10 %` and CJK rate ≤ 10 % (the recipe's own OOD signature); (d) cycle fidelity `cos(AR(AV(v)), v)` mean must exceed `cos(AR(AV(v)), v_other)` mean (real vs foreign row). These are *liveness* checks, not the gate.
  - **H-M3 (new — the depth profile, primary reason to train all layers).** Per layer ℓ, on the W harness (60 items, repaired 471-span anchoring, teacher-forced `G_sum`): `S_c3_ℓ` (single-layer NLA round trip of the clean state, written at ℓ) against `S_swap_ℓ` (raw clean state written at ℓ, the fidelity ceiling at that layer) and `S_random_ℓ`. Prediction, falsifiable: the fidelity ratio `S_c3_ℓ / S_swap_ℓ` is **≥ 0.80 at every layer where the pair is live** (the 12B pair sat at 0.983 at L32); CONFIRM if the per-layer ratio's CI lower bound ≥ 0.80 at ≥ 80 % of live layers; REFUTE if the median ratio across live layers < 0.80. Secondary, descriptive: where in depth `S_swap_ℓ` itself peaks (which layer the clean state is most causally load-bearing at when written at identifier spans) — reported as a table, no verdict word.
  - **Multi-layer arm — the layer set, fixed now from H-W31 (`W31-CONCENTRATED` in the global heads at 41/47 of 48; §5 row 1 of the scoping doc):** primary `M` = **{8, 22, 24}** — 22 is the released-pair-equivalent depth (32/48 → 22/34), 24 is the layer just above the first global layer above it (23), 8 is the early site. Two *secondary* sets, reported alongside and explicitly labelled secondary: depth-spaced **{8, 17, 26}** (the pre-H-W31 default) and **ALL-34**. The gate verdict is read on the primary set only.
  - **The gate (verbatim from the scoping doc §4, unchanged):** Arms, each written at the L1b identifier spans (all layers of the pair written jointly for the multi-layer arms): `M_edit` (multi-layer: read at each layer, apply the *same* English edit, reconstruct, write all), `S_edit_ℓ` (single-layer edit at each layer alone), `M_c3` (multi-layer round trip of the clean state, no edit), `M_random` (random direction at the same positions and layers, norm-matched), `M_foreign` (edit text from a different item), `SELF` (each position's own activation written back at every layer; identity, tol 1.0 nats as banked). Rules, all on summed `G_sum`, cluster bootstrap over items, N_BOOT 10 000, seed 20260724: **Gate 0** `M_c3 / M_prompt-swap ≥ 0.25` with CI excluding 0 (the 12B pair sat at 0.40 of the swap); **H-M1** `M_edit − max_ℓ S_edit_ℓ > 0` with 95 % CI excluding 0, else `M-NO-GAIN`; **H-M2** `M_edit − M_random ≥ 0.30 · M_c3` with CI excluding 0 → `M-EDIT-LIVE`; H-M1 passes but H-M2 fails → `M-GAIN-BUT-GENERIC`; both fail → `M-GENERIC`; **specificity** `M_edit − M_foreign > 0` with CI excluding 0, else the edit is not content-bearing whatever H-M2 says. `max_ℓ S_edit_ℓ` is taken over the **34 single-layer arms** (all trained layers), which makes H-M1 strictly harder than the scoping doc's three-layer version — recorded so it is not read as a relaxation.
  - **Priors, recorded now:** `M-GENERIC` ~60 %, `M-GAIN-BUT-GENERIC` ~25 %, `M-EDIT-LIVE` ~15 % (unchanged from the scoping doc). H-M3: CONFIRM ~55 % — the 12B's 0.983 was at one late layer; early layers (K < 5) have tiny, BOS-dominated norms (mean token norm 52 at index 1 vs 79 000 at index 33) and I expect the AR to be weakest there. On the depth peak: late-middle (ℓ ≈ 20–26), weakly held.
  - **Go/no-go (scoping doc §7), applied before the SFT array:** the 4B's greedy accuracy on the 60 items must fall within **0.633 ± 0.15** (job 384619, `host_traces.py --model gemma4b`); the end-to-end smoke in job 384622 must pass every stage on 40 docs including `LocalAV`/`NLACritic` loading a trained smoke pair.
