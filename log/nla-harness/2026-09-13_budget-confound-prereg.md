- **Hypotheses / what we're testing:** the user's standing question is whether steering the NLA raises accuracy. Job 391968 said no ([`2026-09-13_accuracy-results.md`](2026-09-13_accuracy-results.md)) and the tier ladder bounded the erasure route at ≈ 0 ([`2026-09-13_tier-ladder-results.md`](2026-09-13_tier-ladder-results.md)). **Before adding another steering arm, the instrument itself is suspect.** Re-reading the banked accuracy rows (no GPU): at `steer_run.MAX_NEW_GEN = 1100`, the greedy **parsed rate is 0.800 on `noop` and 0.717–0.800 on every arm** — so ~20 % of generations never emit a parseable answer, and `graded()` scores an unparsed generation as **wrong**. That mass is unreachable by any steering arm: a write that improves comprehension cannot show up in an item that never commits to an answer. CLAUDE.md's own anchor finding is a **~2 048-token System-2 accuracy plateau**, i.e. roughly twice the budget every accuracy run in this programme has used. The parse diagnostics ([`2026-09-13_parse-diagnostics.md`](2026-09-13_parse-diagnostics.md)) already attributed ≈ ⅔ of the tier penalty to output budget → length → parse failure; this asks whether the same mechanism is also suppressing the *steering* contrasts.
  - **H-A8a (primary, instrument check).** Paired per item, greedy, 60 items: `parsed(noop @ 2600) − parsed(noop @ 1100)`. **`BUDGET-BINDING`** if Δ ≥ **+0.05** with the bootstrap CI excluding 0 (McNemar on the parse events reported alongside). **`BUDGET-SATURATED`** otherwise. If `BUDGET-BINDING`, **every accuracy null in job 391968 is labelled budget-confounded in the ledger** and no steering-accuracy claim — positive or negative — is made at 1100 again.
  - **H-A8b (secondary, gated on `BUDGET-BINDING`).** Does the extra budget convert into accuracy? `acc(noop @ 2600) − acc(noop @ 1100)`, paired, McNemar. Descriptive: `acc_given_parsed` at both budgets — if accuracy among parsers is flat while parsed rate rises, the budget was purely a *commitment* limit, not a comprehension one.
  - **H-A8c (the steering question, at the fixed budget).** `c3_band @ 2600 − noop @ 2600`, paired, McNemar, 60 items and the 7 flippable. `c3_band` is chosen because it is the **oracle** (the actual clean state written back) and the only arm that led on the flippable subset (0.536 vs 0.232) — if the budget was the binding constraint, this is where a rescue would become visible. **Pre-declared bound, restated unchanged from job 391968:** acc_l0 0.567 vs acc_l1b 0.533 is 2/60 net headroom with 7 flippable, so the maximum detectable rescue is ≈ +0.03 overall / ≈ +0.10 on the flippable subset, and **no positive significance claim is possible at n = 60** (perfect rescue of all 7 against the measured 6–9/60 same-condition churn gives McNemar p = 0.092). H-A8c is reported with CIs and **no verdict word**, exactly as job 391968's accuracy stage was.
