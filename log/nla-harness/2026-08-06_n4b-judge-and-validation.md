### Target Date: 2026-08-06 (N4b — D3 judge fallback, and the validation that changes N5)
- **Hypotheses / what we're testing:** Instrument validation, with a pre-set gate: do the two independent localizers agree on WHERE a wrong trace first goes wrong? Gate: D3 within ±1 sentence of D2-state on ≥60% of the overlap, else D2-state is capped at confidence 0.30 and barred from burst targeting.
- **Setup:** Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct (cross-family vs the Qwen target and Qwen NLA) via sglang on GPU 3, `--mem-fraction-static 0.55`, temp 0.7 × 3 seeds, median sentence index. 94 cases (76 unlocalized + 18 D2-state validations). New module [`../../nla/src/first_error_judge.py`](../../nla/src/first_error_judge.py) — deliberately separate module/process/output from N7's answer-blind judge, because **D3 sees ground truth**. Output: `data/nla/n4/2026-08-06/first_error_judge.jsonl`.
- **Results:**
  - **91/94 verdicts.** Kinds: misread_code 65 · arithmetic 11 · none 8 · control_flow 5 · assumption 2. Position median 49% of trace (IQR 37–72%).
  - **D3 earns its place on exactly the cases D2 cannot touch.** On `63:L2` — the dispatcher mis-decode where every claimed value is correct — it identifies the true error: *"`EnHOIf['JHrvY'](fibfib, EnHOIf['KOqQU'](14, 0x1))` translates to `fibfib(15)`"*. The model read the call as `fibfib(15)` instead of `fibfib(14)` and then computed **that** program correctly. No value-checker can see this; the judge can.
  - **VALIDATION GATE FAILED.** D3 vs D2-state agreement within ±1 sentence: **7/18 = 39%** (gate ≥60%). Positional disagreement: median Δu **11%** of trace, p75 21%, max 66%. **Correlation between the two position estimates: r = +0.10** — that is, essentially no agreement about where the error is.
  - **D3 is itself only semi-stable:** across its own 3 seeds, spread ≤1 sentence on **42/91 (46%)**; median spread 2 sentences, p90 **20** sentences.
  - **What survives validation:** D2-call **3 cases** (conf 0.90, all three verified by the known-answer test) and D0 slice set-diff **20 cases** (deterministic, no inference). **What does not:** D2-state's 18 (→ conf 0.30, excluded from precise targeting) and D3's 91 (uncorroborated, half seed-unstable).
- **What worked / hypothesis verdict:** Gate **FAILED**, and the failure is informative rather than fatal: **precise first-error localization is unsolved on this corpus outside the recurrence family.** Two independent methods disagree at r≈0.10, and the LLM method disagrees with itself half the time. Neither can be promoted without the other corroborating it.
- **Observations:**
  1. **Design consequence for N5 (must be applied before the run):** a ±20-token burst is only justified for the 3 D2-call cases. Median Δu of 11% is ~70 tokens on a typical trace — wider than the window. **The dense uniform sweep becomes the primary instrument, and the plan's fixed 0.70–0.90 strip becomes the primary "error region" mechanism**, applied to every case rather than to localized ones. Where a localization exists, use a wide region (±15% of trace), not a point.
  2. What N5 can and cannot claim changes accordingly: it can compare wrong vs matched-correct traces at matched relative positions; it **cannot** claim "reads change at the moment of the error" for more than the 3 verified cases, unless a better localizer arrives.
  3. Operational: sglang's constrained-JSON decoding **segfaulted the scheduler** (exit −11) and killed the server mid-run. Replaced with plain generation + lenient JSON extraction (`parse_verdict`), so a malformed reply costs one sample rather than the server. Worth remembering for N7, which was also specified to use constrained decoding.
- **New questions / new hypotheses:** (1) Would a real JS AST instrumentation (acorn via node) raise D2 coverage enough to matter, or is the sentence-level target simply too fine? (2) Is "first error" even well-defined for the misread_code family — the model mis-transcribes the program once and is self-consistent thereafter, so the error is arguably at the *transcription*, which may be nowhere near where values first diverge (the L3 case already shows value-divergence lagging concept-divergence by 2 terms). (3) Would agreement improve at paragraph/step granularity rather than sentence?
- **Next Steps:** (1) apply the N5 design change above; (2) S1 async gate (needs a free GPU); (3) pre-registration entry; (4) N5. Do not use D2-state or D3 localizations for precise targeting until a better validator exists.
